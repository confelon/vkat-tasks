How the models score on a held-out validation year

Training conditions

MAE — mean absolute error on the validation year, in the unit of each value (pressure in hPa). Skill — improvement over climatology: 1 − MAEmodel / MAEclimatology; positive means better than predicting the seasonal average, 0 means no better. Bias — mean signed error (prediction − actual): near zero is healthy, a large value means the model runs systematically high or low even if its MAE looks fine.
